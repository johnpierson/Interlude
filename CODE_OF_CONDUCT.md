# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Argue about the work, not the person doing it.

Most of what follows is elaboration on those three sentences.

## What is expected

- Be welcoming to people whose background, experience or way of working differs from yours.
- Give and take criticism about code gracefully. A review comment is about a patch, not a person.
- Say what you mean plainly, and be willing to be wrong.
- Remember that people contribute to this in gaps between other work. Slow replies are not
  indifference.

## What is not

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, or dismissing someone's question as beneath answering.

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages — and
anywhere someone is representing the project publicly.

## Reporting

Report a problem to **johntpierson@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's
identity is not shared.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that do not follow
this, and will explain why when they do. Repeated or serious behaviour can lead to a temporary or
permanent ban.
